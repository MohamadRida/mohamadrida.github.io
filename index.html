About

I help architecture and engineering practices deliver better-performing buildings. I provide dynamic building simulation, retrofit strategy, and overheating (TM59 / Part O) compliance assessment, backed by a PhD in building physics and over a decade of applied simulation and industry consultancy work, including CFD diagnostics for hospital and heritage clients.

Core capability: dynamic simulation, retrofit strategy for existing housing, overheating and thermal comfort assessment, low-carbon heating system evaluation, and CFD for indoor and industrial environments.

Services

Building Performance Simulation

Dynamic thermal and energy simulation to evaluate design or retrofit options against performance, comfort, and compliance requirements.

Overheating Assessment (TM59 / Part O)

Independent overheating risk assessment to support planning submissions and Part O compliance for residential developments.

Retrofit & Decarbonisation Advisory

Evidence-based evaluation of retrofit measures and low-carbon heating options for existing residential buildings.

Selected Projects

CFD Diagnostics for Industrial Partners

Computational fluid dynamics investigations for industrial partners, diagnosing airflow and thermal performance issues in mechanical systems and translating findings into practical engineering recommendations.

Emergency Ventilation CFD Study

CFD study of emergency ventilation efficiency for a large public building, assessing extraction performance following an accidental chemical spill scenario.

Heated Desk Energy & Comfort Evaluation

Simulation-based evaluation of a heated-desk technology's impact on whole-building energy performance and occupant comfort.

Infrared Heating for Low-Carbon Retrofit

Experimental and simulation study of infrared heating for residential retrofit, using validated dynamic ESP-r models to assess performance across building fabric and occupancy conditions.

Social Housing Retrofit Modelling

Modelling of retrofit strategies for social housing, evaluating heat pump and electrified heating performance under realistic occupancy and cost conditions.
